# Title (short, descriptive, e.g. "Database Technology Choice")

## Status
`Accepted` / `Proposed` / `Deprecated` / `Superseded`

## Context
Why is this decision being made?  
What is the background, constraints, or forces at play?

## Decision
What is the decision?  
State it clearly, with enough detail for someone in the future to understand what you chose.

## Consequences
What are the results of making this decision?  
What will change?  
Are there positive or negative side effects?

## Alternatives Considered (optional but useful)
What other options were considered, and why weren’t they chosen?
